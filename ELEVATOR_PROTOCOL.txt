CO CHE GIAO TIEP ROBOT - THANG MAY

Nguon: src/elevator_robot/elevator_robot/elevator_manager.py

Transport: MQTT, JSON payload.
Broker hien tai cua elevator_manager: 10.11.55.162:1883.
Robot ID hien tai: robot3.
Cac gia tri broker va robot_id can cau hinh lai theo project dich.

GUI -> Manager: ROS 2 /elevator/set_destination (std_msgs/Int32), gia tri la so phong.
Manager -> GUI: ROS 2 /elevator/manager_status (std_msgs/String), JSON trang thai.

GUI LENH
Topic: elevator/request
Publish QoS: 1.
Payload: {"robot_id":"robot3","command":"CALL_ELEVATOR","destination_floor":2}
Lenh: GET_STATUS, CALL_ELEVATOR, OPEN_DOOR, CLOSE_DOOR.
destination_floor chi them khi co tang dich; CALL_ELEVATOR dung ca goi thang den tang robot va dua thang den tang dich.

NHAN PHAN HOI
Subscribe elevator/status khi MQTT ket noi thanh cong, sau do gui GET_STATUS.
Payload vi du: {"current_floor":2,"state":"ARRIVED"}
Callback doc current_floor vao elevator_floor va state vao elevator_state.
Khi state == ARRIVED, cap nhat last_arrived_floor.
Cac trang thai duoc su dung: ARRIVED, DOOR_OPEN, DOOR_CLOSED.
MQTT network loop chay nen bang loop_start().

TRINH TU CHUYEN KHAC TANG
1. Robot den vi tri cho approach_pose.
2. GET_STATUS; code hien tai cho 1 giay de lay trang thai.
3. Neu thang khac tang robot: xoa last_arrived_floor, CALL_ELEVATOR ve tang robot; cho ARRIVED dung tang, timeout 60 giay.
4. OPEN_DOOR; cho DOOR_OPEN, timeout 15 giay.
5. Robot vao cabin tai parking_pose.
6. CLOSE_DOOR; cho DOOR_CLOSED, timeout 15 giay.
7. Xoa last_arrived_floor; CALL_ELEVATOR toi tang dich.
8. Cho ARRIVED dung tang, timeout 120 giay.
9. OPEN_DOOR; cho DOOR_OPEN.
10. Doi map, khoi tao lai AMCL tai parking_pose, robot ra thang va toi phong.
Cac ham cho kiem tra trang thai moi 0.2 giay; timeout tra ve False va luong chuyen bao loi.

PHAM VI VA LUU Y KHI AP DUNG
Repository chi co phia robot; chua thay firmware/PLC/bridge phia thang may nhan lenh, dieu khien cua/dong co, doc cam bien va publish trang thai.
Khong suy dien rang publish MQTT thanh cong la thang da thuc hien lenh; code cho trang thai ung dung.
Code hien tai chua doi chieu request_id, timestamp hoac ACK rieng cho tung lenh; khi port can xem xet phan hoi cu va mat ket noi.
map_switcher.py cung co MQTT nhung broker la 10.11.62.44:1883; khong nham voi broker cua elevator_manager.
